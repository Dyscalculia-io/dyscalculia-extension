Explain any number on a web page: see it in parts, in a place value chart, as number to words, rounded and on a number line. Plus step-by-step percentage, discount, tip, bill split, unit price and time helpers. Free, no account, nothing sent anywhere.

EXPLAIN A NUMBER
• Select a number and press Alt+Shift+N, or right-click → Explain this number.
• In parts: 4,072,500,000 → 4 billion 72 million 500 thousand.
• Place value chart: every digit in a coloured column with its value underneath (1, 10, 100 … 0.1, 0.01).
• Expanded form: 30 + 7 + 0.8 + 0.05.
• Number to words, with read-aloud (English, German, Spanish and Turkish).
• Rounding from coarse to fine, a number line, and for money a breakdown into notes and coins.
• Understands 1,234.56 and 1.234,56, spaces as thousands separators, currency signs and percentages.

TOOLTIPS (OPTIONAL)
• Turn on tooltips and hover over a long number to see it in parts; double-click for the full breakdown.

EVERYDAY MATHS, STEP BY STEP
• Percentage calculator: percent of a number, percent change, discounts.
• Tip calculator and bill split.
• Unit price calculator: which pack is better value, per 100 g, 100 ml or per item.
• Time between two clock times and minutes to hours.
• Every answer shows how it was worked out.

WHO IT IS FOR
People who find long numbers, percentages or prices hard to take in, including people with dyscalculia, and parents and teachers who want to show the steps. It is a helper, not a test or a treatment.

LANGUAGES
English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Nederlands, Português (Brasil), Türkçe.

PRIVACY
No account, no analytics, no tracking. Everything is worked out in your browser.
